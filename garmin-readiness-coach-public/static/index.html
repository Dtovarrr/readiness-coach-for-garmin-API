    Check-ins

    Switch indicators on or off, rename them, or add your own. Thumbs up always means “good for you”.

    Share check-ins with the AI coach
    Off by default. When on, the daily briefing and “Ask the coach” get your ratings (good / neutral / not great) and tags from the last 14 days, plus the patterns found. Your notes are never sent. The coach treats this as wellness context and won’t diagnose.
    Delete all check-in data
    Removes every rating, note and tag from this computer. Your indicator list stays. This can’t be undone.

    Intervals.icu sync

    Sync workouts to Intervals.icu
    Off by default. When on, a workout’s Sync to Intervals.icu button puts it on your Intervals.icu calendar (as a .fit) on its scheduled day. If Intervals.icu is connected to Garmin Connect, it can send it on to your watch. The key is read from .env (INTERVALS_API_KEY, from intervals.icu › Settings › Developer Settings) and is never shown or logged. Garmin login details are never asked for.

    Help

    If things feel heavy, talking with someone you trust can help. In the US you can call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, any time, or chat at 988lifeline.org. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911.

    This app offers wellness and fitness guidance only. It isn’t medical advice and can’t diagnose anything.

    Data & privacy

    • Everything runs on this computer; the server only listens on 127.0.0.1.
    • Goals and settings are stored in .data/; Garmin responses and AI write-ups are cached in .cache/.
    • The coach receives derived numbers only, never your GPS route, location, name or IDs.
    • Map tiles are the only outside requests that reveal an area (the visible tiles).
    • Check-ins stay on this computer; the coach only sees your ratings and tags if you switch sharing on, and never your notes.
    • Photos never leave this computer: location and other metadata are removed on upload, and they are never sent to Claude or any other service.
